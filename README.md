# When-life-gives-you-lemons
Ever have a hard time in life? This blog is about taking things easy and making the best of things with self care and entertainment like books and anime.
I as a personal human being, and being the owner of this blog, can understand life can be challenging when it comes to stress and boredom. That's why as part of 
a side project while im in cybersecurity school, I decided to make a personal blog on my favorite forms of self care, as well as my favorite anime and books. I will also try
to add some commenting features so people can comment and add to the blog of their favorites too!
